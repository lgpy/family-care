Screens v2

What changes from 0.6.0:
  • Pill looks. Every medicine gets a picture of the actual pill: shape, colour (two for capsules), score line, size, or a photo. It appears wherever the medicine does: doses, the give screen, lists, shopping.
  • Icons to scan by. Every section, row type, tab and status has an icon: appointment types, fasting, place, times, prescription, stock.
  • Short forms. One screen instead of steps; chips and steppers instead of typing; only the essentials visible, the rest under "More options" with sensible defaults.
  • Lighter Today. One attention card with compact rows instead of three big blocks.
