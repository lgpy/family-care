  1. Where it lives. A new row in the amber card on Today, only when there are missed doses from earlier days ("4 tomas em falta"). Today's own missed doses stay where they are now, in the day's list. The row opens the new "Tomas em falta" screen.
  2. The list. Unconfirmed doses of the last 7 days, newest day first, one card per patient and time. Each has Foi dada and Não foi dada. A recorded card folds to one line until you leave the screen; tap it to change it. No "mark all" button (decided 2026-11-22).
  3. "Foi dada" opens a short sheet (frame 3) instead of saving straight away. The time it was given defaults to the scheduled time, not now. That matters for stock: if you counted the box since, a dose recorded "now" would be subtracted twice. Who gave it and which medicines (untick to skip some) work like the existing give screen.
  4. "Não foi dada" records a skip straight away, without asking why (decided 2026-11-22).
  5. 7 days is a proposal. Older doses can still be fixed from the medicine's history if needed later; I'd keep the list short so it doesn't become a guilt pile.
  6. Read-only members see the list but no buttons, same as Today.
